About Me

A little bit about the person behind these pages.

Jervis Verano

College Student & Journal Writer

Hi, I’m jervis. I’m a college student who likes technology, creativity, and learning new things. I also have a growing interest in agriculture and how it helps people and the environment. This diary is my space to write about my college life, what I learn, and the experiences I go through each day. I also share my thoughts about technology, simple creative ideas, and things I discover about farming and nature. Each entry shows where I am in my journey right now. It reminds me of my progress, the challenges I face, and how much I continue to grow over time.
